About HomeTurf

Your home for beautiful football since 2020

Our Story

HomeTurf was born from a simple love of the beautiful game. Tired of playing on dusty, uneven fields with poor facilities, we set out to create the ultimate 5-a-side experience right here in our community.

Since opening our doors in 2020, we've hosted thousands of matches — from casual kickabouts with friends to competitive league fixtures. Our mission is simple: give every player access to a professional-quality pitch where they can play their best football.

Whether you're organizing a corporate team-building event, a birthday party, or just a regular weekly game with your mates, HomeTurf is your pitch.

FAQ

Common questions answered

How many players per pitch? +

Our pitch is designed for 5-a-side games (5 players per team, 10 total). However, you can play with fewer players if you prefer. Maximum recommended is 6-a-side.

What should I bring? +

Bring your boots (molded studs or astro turf shoes only — no metal studs), shin guards, and your team. We provide balls, bibs, and goals. Water is available at the cafe.

Can I cancel or reschedule? +

Yes! Cancellations made 24 hours in advance receive a full credit for a future booking. Same-day cancellations forfeit the slot but can reschedule subject to availability.

Do you offer league packages? +

Absolutely! We offer discounted rates for teams that book recurring weekly slots. Contact us via WhatsApp or email for league and team package pricing.

Is there an age limit? +

Players of all ages are welcome! We do ask that children under 16 are supervised by an adult. We also host kids' parties and youth team training sessions.

What happens if it rains? +

Our artificial turf is all-weather and drains quickly. Games go ahead in light rain. In case of severe weather, we'll contact you to reschedule at no extra cost.
